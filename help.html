🔥 Hell Online

Underworld handbook

Hell Online is an entertainment experience inspired by the folk belief in the Eighteen Levels of Hell. None of the sins are real. The Book of Life and Death draws them at random.

A soul's journey

  1. Ox-Head and Horse-Face come to collect your soul.
  2. The Judge reads your sins from the Book of Life and Death. You may claim innocence, but the Karma Mirror never lies.
  3. Pure souls are carried by magic to the stairway to heaven. The angel at the gate destroys any ghost that tries to sneak in.
  4. Guilty souls are taken to King Yama for sentencing, then escorted to the level matching their heaviest sin.
  5. There you play the sinner: endure light, medium and heavy punishment, then repeat in a new scene… until your karma is spent.
  6. Once your sentence is served, you cross the Naihe Bridge, drink Granny Meng's soup to forget your past life, and are reborn on the wheel of reincarnation.

The Eighteen Levels of Hell

👅
Level 1
Hell of Tongue Ripping

For liars and slanderers.

  • Lying without blinking to hurt others
  • Spreading fake news on social media
  • Slandering a colleague to get their job
  • Making sweet promises and never keeping them
✂️
Level 2
Hell of Scissors

For those who break up other people's relationships.

  • Breaking up other people's relationships
  • Talking a friend into leaving their spouse for your own gain
  • Leaking private matters to wreck a marriage
🌲
Level 3
Hell of the Iron Tree

For those who turn siblings against each other.

  • Turning brothers and sisters against each other
  • Fighting over an inheritance until the family stopped speaking
  • Badmouthing your parents to the relatives
🪞
Level 4
Hell of the Karma Mirror

For those who hid their crimes and escaped punishment in life.

  • Lying to the Judge of the Dead
  • Framing an innocent person
  • Using connections to escape a conviction
♨️
Level 5
Hell of the Steamer

For gossips and troublemakers.

  • Making up stories in the group chat
  • Stirring up trouble between neighbours
  • Exaggerating other people's business
🏛️
Level 6
Hell of the Copper Pillar

For arsonists and those who harm others on purpose.

  • Setting fire to someone else's house
  • Causing an accident on purpose for revenge
  • Poisoning the neighbour's livestock
⛰️
Level 7
Mountain of Knives

For those who killed cruelly or for fun.

  • Killing animals for fun
  • Poaching endangered species
  • Tormenting animals before killing them
🏔️
Level 8
Mountain of Ice

For cheaters, gamblers and ungrateful children.

  • Cheating on your partner
  • Gambling your family into ruin
  • Abandoning your elderly parents
🫕
Level 9
Hell of the Oil Cauldron

For thieves, swindlers and false accusers.

  • Stealing from others
  • Running online scams
  • Falsely accusing honest people
  • Snatching bags in the street
🐃
Level 10
Pit of Oxen

For those who abused animals.

  • Abusing pets
  • Dumping cats and dogs on the street
  • Working oxen and buffalo to exhaustion
🪨
Level 11
Hell of Crushing Stones

For those who abandoned or abused children.

  • Abandoning your children
  • Abusing children
  • Neglecting to raise your own children
🥣
Level 12
Hell of the Mortar and Pestle

For those who wasted food.

  • Throwing away food while others went hungry
  • Complaining about food and knocking over the whole tray
  • Ordering lots of dishes just for photos, then leaving them
🩸
Level 13
Pool of Blood

For those who disrespected ancestors and elders.

  • Disrespecting your ancestors and neglecting their graves
  • Insulting the elderly
  • Being rude to your teachers
⚰️
Level 14
Hell of Wasted Lives

For those who treated life, theirs or others, as cheap.

  • Drunk driving that killed someone
  • Street racing
  • Doing dangerous stunts for likes
🪓
Level 15
Hell of Dismemberment

For grave robbers and those who insulted the dead.

  • Robbing graves
  • Vandalising temples and shrines
  • Insulting the dead
🌋
Level 16
Mountain of Fire

For the corrupt and those who took bribes.

  • Taking bribes
  • Skimming money from charity
  • Embezzling public funds
⚙️
Level 17
Hell of the Millstone

For abusers of power and fake holy men.

  • Abusing power to oppress ordinary people
  • Posing as a monk to swindle money
  • Hypocrisy: preaching one thing and doing another
🪚
Level 18
Hell of the Saw

For cheating merchants.

  • Rigging scales and short-changing customers
  • Selling counterfeit goods
  • Tax evasion and cooking the books

Connecting to Life Online

A life simulation site can send a soul here with the ?soul=… parameter (base64url-encoded JSON with a name, a list of sins and merit). The Judge then reads the recorded sins instead of drawing random ones.
